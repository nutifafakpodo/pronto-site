FAQ

Questions, answered.

Something else on your mind? Drop us a line.

What kinds of businesses is Pronto for?

Any business that runs on appointments: salons, barbers, spas, nail and beauty studios, clinics, therapists, personal trainers, fitness studios, coaches, consultants and tutors — whether it’s just you or a whole team.

Do my customers need an account to book or pay?

No. Customers book from your page with their name, email and phone number, and pay invoices straight from the link you send. They also get a calendar file to add the appointment to their own calendar.

How do invoices and payments work?

Create an invoice from any appointment in one tap — the service, price and customer are filled in for you. Send it by email, SMS or WhatsApp with a secure payment link. When the customer pays, the invoice is marked paid and a receipt goes out automatically.

Can I put the booking page on my website or Instagram?

Yes. Share your link anywhere — Instagram, WhatsApp, Google Business, a QR code at the front desk — or embed the booking page directly on your own website.

What happens when a customer cancels or reschedules?

Every confirmation and reminder includes a cancellation link. You’re notified straight away and the slot opens back up for someone else. If you move a booking, the customer is told automatically.

Will it work with the calendar I already use?

Yes. You and your staff can connect Google Calendar or Outlook through their official sign-in — Pronto never sees your password. Personal events block your availability so you’re never double-booked.

Can I use Pronto on my phone?

Pronto works in any modern browser on a computer, tablet or phone, so you can run the front desk from wherever you are. Native iOS and Android apps are in development.
