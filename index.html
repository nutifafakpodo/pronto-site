How it works

Say what you mean. Pronto handles the rest.

No forms, no dropdowns, no hunting for the right calendar. Just tell Pronto what you need, the way you’d tell a person.

  1. 1

    Say it your way

    Type “coffee with Ama next Tuesday at 10” — or hold the mic and just say it. Space bar works too.

    coffee with Ama next Tuesday at 10
  2. 2

    Pronto understands

    It pulls out the title, time, people, place and repeat rules — and tells you how sure it is.

    What Coffee with Ama When Tue · 10:00 AM Who Ama Boateng Sure 94%
  3. 3

    Confirm, and it’s done

    Pronto checks for clashes, picks the right calendar, and creates the event. Invites go out as usual.

    Added to Outlook · Ama invited

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Something else on your mind? Drop us a line.

Which calendars does Pronto work with?

Google Calendar and Microsoft Outlook today, and you can connect more than one account. Apple Calendar support is on the way.

Do I have to switch away from my current calendar?

No. Pronto works on top of the calendars you already use. Events are created in your real calendar, so they show up on your phone, laptop and anywhere else as usual.

How does Pronto access my calendar?

Through Google’s and Microsoft’s official sign-in. Pronto never sees your password, and you can disconnect a calendar at any time from Settings — which also removes its events from Pronto.

What if Pronto gets something wrong?

Nothing is booked until you confirm it. Every request shows a preview with a confidence score, and Pronto flags anything it isn’t sure about so you can edit it first.

How does voice input work?

Hold the mic button — or the space bar — and speak for up to a minute. Your words appear as text so you can check them before Pronto schedules anything.

Do my customers need a Pronto account to book?

No. With Pronto for Business, customers book from your public page with just their name, email and phone number, and get a calendar file to add the appointment to their own calendar.

Is there a mobile app?

Pronto works in any modern browser today. Native iOS and Android apps are in development.
